Terms & Conditions

1. 791 Emergency Service Scope

The 791 Mother Emergency Application provides a dedicated clinical triage and rapid emergency communication service for expectant mothers in coordination with regional healthcare centers and delivery units.

2. Health Data Protection (GDPR & Kanta)

All maternal and fetal health metadata transmitted via this application complies with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and national healthcare client data processing regulations. All data exchanges are end-to-end encrypted.

3. Emergency Dispatch & Geolocation

When activating the 10-second SOS countdown, location coordinates are shared with emergency responders to assist in rapid medical dispatch and clinic navigation.

4. Medical Disclaimer

In critical, life-threatening scenarios where cellular or data connectivity is unavailable, the user must immediately call the national emergency number 112 directly.
