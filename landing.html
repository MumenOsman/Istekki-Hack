Instructions for Usage (Click to collapse/expand)
1
Midwife Duty: On Midwife app, log in (502941 / password123) and tap the green button to enter Listening mode.
2
Mother SOS: Tap the red SOS button on the Mother app to trigger emergency call and transmit telemetry.
3
Answer Call: Tap Accept on the Midwife app to establish voice connection, request video, or view EHR report.
4
Escalate to ED: Use Midwife triage dock to request Ambulance (Code A) or Observation (Code C); confirm dispatch on ED console.
5
End Call: Tap Hang Up on either app to terminate the session and reset all apps to standby.
